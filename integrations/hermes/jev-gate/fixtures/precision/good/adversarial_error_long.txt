Analysis: error rates and model reliability.

This brief covers three public reliability reports. The first compares provider error rates across a week of production traffic and notes that timeout handling matters more than raw model quality for long-running jobs. The second describes a team that failed to produce a stable evaluation harness until it moved to fixed fixtures; the failure is the subject of the report, not the state of this digest. The third documents a rollback after a bad prompt change and the checks that caught it.

All three items include dates and source links. The brief does not claim that any provider is universally reliable, and it does not generalize from a small sample. A short limitations section explains that the evidence is observational.
